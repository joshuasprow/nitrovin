The part type in the strip

A proposal for #366. The row of part types and its "N more" dialog become one field in the "Parts that fit" strip: the model and the year, and under them the part type, from the catalog.

Nothing pressed One shape at every width. The strip names the model and the year; under them, in the same tape, is the field, never wider than 24rem. It reads "Choose a part type" with the vehicle's count, and the list starts open under the strip, grouped by the part vocabulary, with a filter.
