Swatches: in use

What the page is built from today, as pkg/ui/static/ui.css defines it and DESIGN.md describes it: its materials, then the marks and fittings made of them. A chip is the stylesheet's own value. These are settled.

Brushed aluminum

metal

The plate the VIN is struck on, the brand, a dialog's head, and every key: a button is a key of this metal, raised on a steel ledge.

A fine horizontal brush over a light-to-mid grey gradient; characters are struck in, lit below and shadowed above. The same in both colour schemes.

base #cdd5dcink #232930accent #6f7b86

ui.css: --metal, --on-metal, --steel
