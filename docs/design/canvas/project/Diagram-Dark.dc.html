Decoded cards, brackets and lines

Each card is a manila parts tag, hung by its eyelet from a line of pinstripe tape in its group's colour. Manila is a third material: like the metal and the tape, it keeps its colours in both schemes.

Decoded Paper stock with grain, clipped corners, a reinforced eyelet, the group's colour printed as a band. Brackets and lines are tape, lit on one edge and lifted off the ground. A control on a tag is label tape in the tag's colour.
