Cards, brackets and lines

Each card is a manila tag hung by its eyelet from its group's line. A row is a label over its value, both left-aligned. Under the cards, the vehicle's block of label tape says what the vehicle is, and the Maker, Vehicle and Model year lines end on it. One trunk runs down from the block to the part type picker, and the vehicle's names hang from the block beside the picker as manila tags, a vocabulary each.

Decoded Brackets, leader lines, cards, the lines to the block, the block, the trunk to the picker and the names. Nothing is pressed, so a blank strip asks for a part type.
