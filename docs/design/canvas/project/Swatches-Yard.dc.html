Swatches: the yard and the shelf

What parts and vehicles are tagged, labelled and marked with in a yard, a warehouse and a shop. Found by a web search on 2026-11-20; every source was opened and checked against its card on 2026-11-21. A link with nothing after it is a page read that states a fact of the card. "In part": the page is about the material and states none of the card's facts. "Summary": the page refused, and a search result's summary was read instead. "Unread": neither could be read. Dead and unrelated pages are gone from the cards. Every colour is an estimate, since no source publishes one, and no recipe here is settled.
