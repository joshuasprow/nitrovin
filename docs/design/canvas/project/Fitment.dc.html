The fitment card

The parts that fit: the vehicle's block, which says what the vehicle is, its names as manila tags, a picker of the catalog's part types, and the candidates as manila tags under the picker, where the lines end. A candidate's stock strip feeds out its shelf and yard rows, a receipt on thermal paper.

Picker, candidates, marks and stock Alternator pressed: the picker names it and counts its candidates, and × beside it clears. Under its tape, what the decode says of this vehicle, to match each candidate's options against. The first candidate's stock is open under its row.
