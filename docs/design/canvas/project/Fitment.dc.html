The fitment card

The parts that fit. Under the vehicle's block the page is a counter: a picker of the catalog's part types with the vehicle's names beside it, then the candidates as rows down the page, each a manila tag laid long and tied by a stub to the one trunk that runs down from the block. A candidate's stock strip feeds out its shelf and yard rows, a print in columns on thermal paper.

Picker, candidates and stock Alternator pressed: the picker names it and counts its candidates, and × beside it clears. On the block, what the decode says of this vehicle, to match each candidate's options against. A candidate is a row: its number on label tape with its years, its option text over its mark and its note, and its stock strip at its trailing end. The first candidate's stock is open under its row. The shelf's header declares the stock number and the condition, and the yard's the stock number and the row; the colour, which it leaves out, is folded.
