Cards, brackets and lines

Each card is a manila tag hung by its eyelet from its group's line. A row is a label over its value, both left-aligned. Under the cards, the vehicle's block of label tape takes the Vehicle and Model year lines and says what the vehicle is; its names hang under it as manila tags, a vocabulary each, in a row that scrolls sideways.

Decoded Brackets, leader lines, cards, join lines, the vehicle's block and its names.
