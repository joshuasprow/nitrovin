Swatches: thermal paper

Direct-thermal paper and its print, as a counter's receipts and a yard's part tags come off the printer. Found by a web search on 2026-11-20. Dimensions and conventions are the sources'; every colour is an estimate, since no source publishes one, and no recipe here is settled.

Fresh direct-thermal receipt paper drawn: receipt

thermal

Counter receipts and pick tickets torn from an 80 mm or 58 mm roll at a parts counter.

Very smooth, plastic-like coated face with a faint satin sheen and almost no visible fibre; spec sheets give about 85-87% brightness, so it is a cool off-white rather than pure white (hex is an estimate).

Heat-darkened thermal paper

thermal

A ticket left on a dashboard or under a hot cup, or scuffed by a fingernail, where the coating has developed on its own.

Cloudy grey bloom and dark scuff streaks over the off-white face; stray heat and friction develop the leuco dye and obscure the print (hexes are estimates).

Thermal print with dead-dot streaks

thermal

Tickets from a worn counter printer, where the same thin vertical white lines run through every line of print.

One-dot-wide (0.125 mm at 203 dpi) unprinted vertical lines at fixed positions down the whole ticket, from failed or dirty print-head elements; overlay this on top of the text layer.
